Our Services

Sendly provides document preparation, printing and pre-mailing support for institutions that need reliable execution before postal submission.

Document Formatting & Data Preparation

We assist in structuring and formatting documents to ensure clarity, consistency, and print readiness.

  • Page size and margin setup so letters fit the envelope
  • Mail-merge of names and addresses from Excel into letters and labels
  • Address placement and legibility check
  • Data format check before bulk preparation

Printing & Folding

  • Black & white and colour printing
  • Standard institutional paper formats
  • Folding or flattening as required
  • Batch-wise execution for volume accuracy
  • Sample proof approval before the full run

Printing and folding are carried out through our print partner, under our supervision.

Pre-Mailing Preparation

  • Envelope insertion
  • Address labelling and placement alignment
  • Sorting and bundling as per requirement
  • Batch tagging for traceability

Dispatch Readiness Support

  • Final check of every batch before handover
  • Bundling and segregation for postal submission
  • Confirmation details shared with you for your records

Postage, booking, delivery and tracking are handled through standard postal channels and are not part of Sendly's service.

Who We Serve

  • Government and administrative offices
  • Banks and financial institutions
  • CA, tax and legal practices
  • Educational institutions
  • Housing societies and associations
  • Corporate offices

Services Not Included

To maintain clarity of scope, the following are not part of Sendly’s services:

  • Content authorship or legal drafting
  • Final approval of document content
  • Postal booking or acceptance
  • Postal tariff determination
  • Delivery guarantees or tracking assurance
